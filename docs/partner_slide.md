# Slide: what a dynamic time-of-use tariff did in the Low Carbon London trial (2013)

- Public data: 862 households on dynamic prices, 3389 on a flat rate, half-hourly readings for 2012 and 2013
- Bills at own tariff: -2.40 £ per household-month, 95 % CI [-3.06, -1.73], -6.0 % of the 2012 bill
- Use in High-price half-hours: -1.46 kWh per month, -10.0 %, with a failed pre-trend test, so read with care
- Total use -3.3 %, Low-price use 1.9 % (not distinguishable from zero)
- Placebos: a false July 2012 treatment date gives nothing, 0 of 500 (p < 0.002) random relabellings match the bill effect
- Design: pre-registered and hash-frozen plan, difference-in-differences with household and month fixed effects, household-clustered errors
- The trial was opt-in, not randomised, with cash incentives, so the difference-in-differences is the headline and the ANCOVA check is the comparison that would have been primary under random assignment
- For a pilot: pre-register before the switch, a year of pre-switch readings per household, randomise the switch if possible
